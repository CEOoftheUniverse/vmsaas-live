The review gap problem

90% of customers read reviews before buying, but only 5-10% leave them voluntarily. AI agents ask at the perfect moment — when satisfaction is highest.

Automated review responses

AI generates personalized responses to every review within minutes. Positive reviews get thank-yous with upsell suggestions. Negative reviews get empathetic, solution-oriented replies.

AI categorizes feedback into themes — service speed, quality, pricing, staff. Dashboard shows exactly what customers love and what needs fixing.

Review-to-improvement pipeline

AI doesn't just collect reviews — it creates actionable improvement tickets from recurring negative themes and tracks resolution impact on future reviews.
