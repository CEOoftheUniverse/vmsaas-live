Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel my annual plan early?

Yes, under our planned terms. Once billing is live, you can cancel at any time from the customer portal. If you cancel within the first 30 days, you receive a full refund. After 30 days, your plan continues until the end of your annual billing period.

Can I upgrade mid-year?

Yes, under our planned terms. Upgrades are prorated. If you upgrade from Base to Swarm 6 months into your annual plan, you pay the prorated difference for the remaining 6 months.

Is the price locked in?

Yes. Once you subscribe, your price is locked for the full 12-month term even if we raise prices. On renewal, the new pricing applies.

Do I get all the same features as monthly?

Yes — same features, same shared GPU-hour allowances, same model access. The only difference is 20% off the price and a 12-month billing cycle.

What payment methods are accepted?

Checkout is not open yet. When it opens, we plan to take major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) through Stripe, and to offer invoices for annual Enterprise plans.
