GPU rental prices, November 2026

Prices checked on 5 Nov 2026, 15:30-16:25 UTC · MoltBot Cloud

TL;DR

  • On-demand H100s cost from USD 1.99 per GPU-hour (RunPod Community Cloud) to USD 12.29 (Azure ND96isr H100 v5, East US). The lowest Vast.ai marketplace listing was USD 1.8675 (all hosts, 15:30 UTC).
  • For market reference, the Silicon Data H100 rental index stood at USD 2.97 on 5 Nov 2026, and Kalshi's H100 monthly contract settled October at USD 2.94.
  • A100 80GB on-demand prices ran from USD 0.845 (Vast.ai, all hosts) to USD 4.10 (Azure). RTX 4090 ran from USD 0.1343 (Vast.ai) to USD 0.89 (RunPod Secure Cloud). RTX 3090 ran from USD 0.090 (Salad, lowest priority) to USD 0.50 (RunPod Secure Cloud).
  • A month is about 730 hours. One H100 rented around the clock costs USD 1,452.70 at RunPod's community rate and USD 5,022.40 at AWS's p5 per-GPU rate.

These are public GPU rental prices we checked on 5 November 2026. We read each provider's price page and, where one exists, its public price API. Every row names the GPU, the type of price and the time we saw it, and links to the source.

Prices change daily, and marketplace listings can change within minutes. Treat each figure as a dated snapshot, not a quote. All prices are in USD per GPU-hour.

How to read GPU prices

The Type column says what kind of price each row is. Here is what the terms mean.

  • On-demand. A fixed hourly price with no commitment. The GPU is yours until you stop it. Most rows above are on-demand prices.
  • Spot, interruptible and preemptible. Spare capacity at a lower price, but the provider can stop your job at short notice. The lowest Vast.ai interruptible H100 SXM bid was USD 0.667-1.000 an hour (16:08-16:21 UTC), against USD 1.8675 for the lowest on-demand listing (15:30 UTC). If you use these prices, save checkpoints often.
  • Priority tiers. Salad sells the same GPU at four priority levels. An RTX 4090 costs USD 0.440 an hour at High priority and USD 0.160 at Lowest. Lower tiers can be preempted.
  • Reserved. You commit to a term for a lower hourly rate. Together AI's H100 costs USD 3.99 on demand, USD 3.69 reserved for 3-30 days and USD 3.19 for 91-180 days.
  • Per GPU or per node. AWS, Azure and CoreWeave sell several of these GPUs as 8-GPU instances, also called nodes. We divide the instance price by 8, so every row compares one GPU. You still rent the whole node: AWS's p5.48xlarge costs USD 55.040 an hour for 8 H100s, which is USD 6.88 per GPU-hour.
  • Lowest and median. A marketplace lists many hosts at different prices. The lowest price is one listing, and the median shows a typical price (see how we checked). "All hosts" includes unverified hosts; in our snapshot, verified hosts cost more.
  • Indexes and settled markets. Silicon Data's index and Kalshi's settled monthly averages summarise a market. They are good reference points, but you cannot rent a GPU at them.

How we checked

  • We checked every price on 5 Nov 2026 between 15:30 and 16:25 UTC. We read each provider's price page, plus the public price APIs of Vast.ai, RunPod, Azure and Kalshi.
  • Prices are in USD per GPU-hour. For instances with several GPUs (AWS, Azure, CoreWeave), we divided the instance price by the number of GPUs.
  • Vast.ai "all hosts" includes unverified hosts. Medians count each machine once, at its cheapest per-GPU offer, weighted by its GPU count; this matches Vast.ai's own price feed.
  • Vast.ai prices include a 10 GB disk. H100 supply was thin (6-8 machines), so these figures can move within minutes. The notes give verified-host figures.
  • Vast.ai's feed groups 40 GB cards into its "A100 80 GB" rows. Our A100 80GB rows therefore come from our own API snapshot, filtered to 80 GB cards.
  • RunPod prices are fixed list prices, so the lowest and typical prices are the same. At 16:11 UTC, RunPod's API showed no single-GPU stock for RTX 3090, H100 PCIe or A100 PCIe.
  • Lambda's public prices take effect on 16 Nov 2026. Until then, its current prices are shown only after login.
  • For Silicon Data we quote the "As of Nov 5, 2026" headline values; the FAQ text on its pages is older.
  • Kalshi contracts settle on the average of hourly values reported by Ornn. kalshi.com blocks automated link checkers, so we read the settled values from Kalshi's public API (H100 series, A100 series).
  • We left out TensorDock: its own pages show conflicting prices, and live prices need a login.
  • This is a list of sources, not an index. We show each source's figures with attribution and do not combine them. Vast.ai's data is free for display and citation with attribution (see the licence in its public price feed). Silicon Data and Kalshi values are quoted with attribution.

From rented GPUs to GPUs in orbit

Cost is not the same as price. The panel's cost model (5-year chips, 8% cost of capital, no margin, 1.2 kW per GPU slot) puts a GPU slot at an Earth hyperscale site at USD 1.62 per GPU-hour of cost. The same slot in low Earth orbit costs USD 8.53 at today's launch price of USD 3,600/kg. It still costs USD 2.85 at USD 30/kg (the panel's model). Chips are about 65% of the cost on Earth and electricity only 5-10%, so free sunlight cannot pay for launch.

SemiAnalysis models USD 8.64 per GPU-hour in orbit against USD 2.37 on the ground (a 16-GPU B300 cluster; projection, 3 Jun 2026).

Starcloud has run an NVIDIA H100 in orbit since November 2025. Crusoe plans to run Crusoe Cloud on a Starcloud satellite, with limited GPU capacity, from early 2027 (Crusoe announcement).

FAQ

How much does it cost to rent an H100 in November 2026?

On 5 Nov 2026, on-demand H100 prices ran from USD 1.99 per GPU-hour on RunPod Community Cloud to USD 12.29 on Azure (ND96isr H100 v5, East US). Vast.ai marketplace listings started at USD 1.8675 (all hosts, 15:30 UTC), and the Silicon Data H100 rental index stood at USD 2.97.

Why do GPU rental prices differ so much?

The type of price matters most: marketplace, interruptible and spot prices are lower, but the GPU can stop or disappear at short notice. Instance size (1 or 8 GPUs), region, host verification and support also change the price.

Is this a GPU price index?

No. It is a dated list of public prices, each linked to its source. We do not combine sources into a composite index.
