FAQ

What GPU do I get?

Plans share a GPU pool instead of reserving a card per customer. Base: up to 60 GPU-hours a month on 24 GB-class GPUs (RTX A5000, 3090 or 4090 class). Swarm: 120 hours plus 5 H100-hours, priority queue. Enterprise: 240 hours plus 10 H100-hours, top priority. Dedicated GPUs cost market rate plus 10%: from USD 0.18 an hour (24 GB) or USD 2.19 (H100) (RunPod rates, November 2026). See how GPU time works.

What counts as a GPU-hour?

One GPU for one hour while your job holds it. Two GPUs for 30 minutes also count as one GPU-hour. Hours reset each month and do not roll over. We plan to show your usage in the customer portal.

What if I need more GPU time?

Extra pool hours and dedicated GPUs cost the market rate plus 10% per GPU-hour: from USD 0.18 an hour for a 24 GB card or USD 2.19 for an H100 (RunPod Community Cloud rates, 5 Nov 2026). Extra time is opt-in and quoted before you buy.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Once checkout opens, you can pay monthly or yearly and cancel anytime. No contracts, no penalties.

What AI models are included?

7 AI model families: Claude, GPT, Gemini, Qwen, Kimi, Grok and DeepSeek. Base uses Qwen 3.5 and Claude Sonnet 4; Swarm and Enterprise can use all 7. Smart routing picks a suitable model for each task. Model API use has fair-use limits, which we will publish before checkout opens.

How is this different from running my own OpenClaw?

We handle GPU provisioning, model API keys, monitoring, backups, and updates. You focus on building — we handle infrastructure.
