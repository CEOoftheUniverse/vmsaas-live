How to Run AI Agents 24/7: GPU Cloud Hosting Compared (2026)

In the rapidly evolving world of artificial intelligence, the ability to run AI agents 24/7 has become crucial. Here we compare five leading GPU cloud hosting options for 2026.

Vast.ai: The Affordable Marketplace

Vast.ai is a decentralized marketplace connecting users with GPU owners. On 5 Nov 2026 (15:30 UTC) its live price list showed H100 SXM rentals from USD 1.87 an hour on demand (median USD 2.39, all hosts) and RTX 3090s from USD 0.09. Interruptible H100 bids started at about USD 0.67-1.00 an hour (16:08-16:21 UTC, Vast.ai console). Reliability varies by host, and interruptible instances can stop at any time, so checkpoint your work.

RunPod: Reliable and Consistent

RunPod rents GPUs at fixed hourly list prices, billed per second. In November 2026 RunPod rented an RTX A5000 (24 GB) for USD 0.16 an hour and an H100 PCIe for USD 1.99 an hour on Community Cloud. Check its terms for uptime commitments before you rely on them for production agents.

Salad.com: Ultra-Cheap Distributed Computing

Salad uses idle consumer GPUs. Its lowest-priority batch tier starts at USD 0.015 an hour for RTX 3060-class cards (Salad, November 2026). Best for batch processing and work that can wait.

Vagon: Cloud Desktop for AI

Vagon provides cloud desktops with GPUs and a user-friendly interface, from USD 0.99 an hour for an NVIDIA T4 cloud computer, plus a storage plan from USD 7.99 a month (Vagon, 5 Nov 2026). Good for interactive AI work, but not the cheapest for always-on agents.

CoreWeave: Enterprise Scale

CoreWeave targets enterprises. In November 2026 it listed an H100 at USD 6.16 an hour on demand (USD 2.46 spot), and an L40 at USD 1.25 (CoreWeave). Best for large-scale training and teams that need reserved capacity.
